Use the `smallest-divisor` procedure to find the smallest divisor of each of the following numbers: 199, 1999, 19999.

## Solution

The smallest divisor for 199, 1999, and 19999 is 199, 1999, and 7 respectively. This means that 199, and 1999 are prime numbers.
